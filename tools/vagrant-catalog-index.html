AlmaLinux OS Vagrant boxes

Official AlmaLinux OS boxes for libvirt, VirtualBox, VMware, Hyper-V and Parallels, on x86_64 and aarch64, built and tested by the AlmaLinux Cloud SIG. Point Vagrant at this catalog once, then use the boxes by name as before.
